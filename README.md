# Stock Market Performance & Sector Analysis

## About the Project

The stock market changes every day, and stock prices can rise or fall depending on market conditions and trading activity. Understanding these changes can help us explore stock performance, price movements, trading volume, and volatility.

In this project, I used Power BI to analyze stock market data and explore the performance of different stocks and sectors. I focused on comparing opening and closing prices, identifying price changes, analyzing trading volume, and understanding how volatility varies across different stocks and months.

## Project Objectives

The main objective of this project was to understand stock market performance through data analysis. I worked on analyzing average, maximum, and minimum closing prices, comparing price changes, studying trading volume, and identifying bullish and bearish price trends. I also explored monthly market patterns, daily price ranges, and volatility across different stocks and categories.

## Dataset Description

The project uses a dataset named `Stockslive.csv`. It contains stock market information such as date, stock symbol, opening price, closing price, highest price, lowest price, and trading volume. These columns help analyze daily price movements, trading activity, and differences in stock performance.

## Tools and Technologies Used

I used **Power BI** for data preparation, calculations, analysis, and dashboard creation. Power Query was used to check missing values and verify data types. DAX was used to create calculated measures, while calculated columns helped categorize price trends, volatility levels, and trading volume.

## Work Performed

I started by importing the stock market dataset into Power BI and checking the data for missing values and correct data types. Next, I created measures to calculate average, maximum, and minimum closing prices, price changes, average daily range, and trading volume.

I also created calculated columns for year, month, day, price trend, volatility, volatility level, volume category, daily return, price range, and moving average comparison.

To explore stock market performance, I created KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, donut charts, pie charts, column charts, scatter plots, area charts, and stacked bar charts. These visualizations helped compare stock symbols, examine monthly price movements, analyze trading volume, and identify periods of higher and lower volatility.

## Key Learnings

Through this project, I gained practical experience in Power BI, data cleaning, DAX measures, calculated columns, and dashboard development. I also improved my understanding of stock price analysis, trading volume, price changes, bullish and bearish trends, and volatility analysis.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how Power BI can be used to explore stock market performance and sector-related patterns. By analyzing stock prices, trading volume, price ranges, and volatility, I learned how to organize financial data and present it through meaningful visualizations. Overall, this project strengthened my data analysis and dashboard development skills.
